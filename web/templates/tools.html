💡 SEO Pro Tips

  • Single H1: Every page should have exactly one prominent H1 tag that encapsulates the primary topic.
  • CTR Boost: Include power words and current year (2026) in your title tags.
  • SERP Limits: Desktop titles truncate at ~60 chars; mobile titles cut at ~55 chars.
  • Long-Tail: Target low-competition keywords to rank on page 1 faster.
